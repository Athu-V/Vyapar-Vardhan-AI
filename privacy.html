Commitment to Data Privacy: Vyapar Vardhan AI is dedicated to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and privacy of information provided by rural micro-entrepreneurs and business operators in compliance with applicable Indian data protection frameworks, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to deliver conversational business advisory, monthly profit and loss diagnosis, and financial structuring calculations:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, encrypted authentication identifiers, or authorized Google OAuth tokens.
  • Business Financial Inputs: Monthly gross revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenditures, debt service commitments, and business trade categories entered voluntarily during diagnostic sessions.
  • Advisory Interactions: Advisory chat logs and calculation parameters stored securely to maintain continuity across sessions.

2. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Executing deterministic financial viability calculations (such as DSCR, Net Margin, and Working Capital cycle assessments).
  • Matching business profiles against public eligibility guidelines for state and central credit-linked subsidy schemes (such as PMEGP, MUDRA, and PMFME).
  • Preserving your operational records across authenticated sessions.

3. Data Storage and Security

All authentication records and advisory data are stored on Google Cloud Firebase infrastructure with enterprise-grade encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We enforce strict least-privilege security rules on cloud databases.

4. Non-Disclosure and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your financial records to advertising networks or unauthorized third parties. Financial metrics entered into the system are never shared with credit rating agencies or lenders without your explicit confirmation.

5. AI Advisory Disclaimer

Vyapar Vardhan AI provides informational assistance and deterministic preliminary calculations based on official scheme guidelines. Outputs do not constitute formal banking sanction letters or accredited financial auditing. All loan sanctions remain subject to bank appraisal and government verification.

6. User Rights and Data Erasure

In accordance with data protection regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access your stored diagnostic reports and conversation history.
  • Request correction of inaccurate business profile data.
  • Request permanent erasure of your account and all associated Firestore records.

7. Contact and Inquiries

For data protection inquiries or to request permanent record deletion, contact the administrative desk at support@vyaparvardhan.ai.
